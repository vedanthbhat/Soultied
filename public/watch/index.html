Soultied · Watch party

Watch Netflix, Prime Video and more together

Wherever you both are: play, pause and skipping stay in sync, with a chat, reactions and a little pixel couch in the corner of the show. No account needed.

Add to Chrome Free. Chrome, Edge or Brave on a computer.

How it works

  1. Add Soultied to your browser (it’s free).
  2. On Netflix or Prime Video, hover any show and press Watch together. On the other services, start the show and press the Watch together tab on the right of the player. Your watch party starts and its link is copied.
  3. Send the link to your person. When they open it, they’re on the same show with you, in sync.

You each need your own account on the service you watch: no video passes between you, only play, pause and your chat. In Edge, allow extensions from other stores when it asks.
