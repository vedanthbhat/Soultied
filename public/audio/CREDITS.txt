The other records on the shelf. Each song is downloaded from where its artist
shared it, re-encoded to a 96 kbps MP3 with its volume evened out to match
the jazz record, and made exactly as long as the record player counts it
(a fraction of a second trimmed or added at the end, with a short fade).
Nothing else was changed (scripts/shelf-audio.mjs).
